# Caller Psychology — Detection & Response

## Behavior-Aware Conversation Design
Not manipulation. Not fake urgency. Behavior-aware adaptation.

## Detection Signals → Agent Response

### Rushed Caller
**Signals:** Short sentences, "quick question", "real quick", talks fast, interrupts
**Adapt:** Skip pleasantries. Direct answers. No upsell. "Yes we have it, $99.99, 2 left."

### Confused Caller
**Signals:** Long pauses, "um", "I'm not sure", asks same question differently
**Adapt:** Slow down. One option at a time. "Let me narrow it down — what's the occasion?"

### Skeptical Caller
**Signals:** "Are you sure?", "Really?", questions pricing, compares to other stores
**Adapt:** Use specifics. "We have 4 bottles of E.H. Taylor at $169.99, it's allocated so most stores are higher."

### Price-Sensitive
**Signals:** "How much is...", "anything cheaper?", "that's a lot", hesitation after price
**Adapt:** Lead with value. Offer alternatives. "For something similar at a better price point, Penelope Four Grain at $30.99 punches way above its weight."

### Premium Buyer
**Signals:** Asks for "best", "top shelf", "rare", "allocated", doesn't ask about price first
**Adapt:** Premium tone. Exclusive language. "We just got 4 bottles of Don Julio 1942. Most stores can't get it right now."

### Annoyed / Angry
**Signals:** Raised voice, "this is ridiculous", "I've been trying to...", complaints
**Adapt:** Acknowledge first. "I completely understand. Let me fix this right now." Then action.

### Ready to Buy
**Signals:** "I'll take it", "can you hold one?", "how do I order?", asks about payment
**Adapt:** Confirm and close. "Great choice. I'll set that aside for you. Can I get your name?"

### Browsing / No Urgency
**Signals:** "Just looking", "what do you have?", open-ended questions, no specific product
**Adapt:** Consultative. Plant seeds. "If you're exploring bourbon, our Angel's Envy Private Selection at $95.99 is getting a lot of attention right now."

## Retention Playbook Examples

### "That's too expensive"
BAD: "Would you like something cheaper?"
GOOD: "If you want the same premium feel without that price tag, I can give you two options right now — one smoother, one better value. Which direction?"

### "You're out of what I want"
BAD: "Sorry, we don't have that."
GOOD: "Not at this moment. I can do three things: check if something comparable is in stock, add you to priority callback when it arrives, or connect you with the owner for alternatives."

### "I'll think about it"
BAD: "OK, thanks for calling!"
GOOD: "Totally understand. Want me to hold it for 24 hours while you decide? We only have 2 left."

### "I saw it cheaper elsewhere"
BAD: "We can't match prices."
GOOD: "Where did you see that? I want to make sure you're comparing the same thing — we see a lot of stores listing prices without including tax or shipping."

### "My order is late"
BAD: "I'm sorry for the inconvenience."
GOOD: "I understand why that's frustrating. Let me pull up your order right now so I can tell you exactly what happened and what the fastest fix is."
